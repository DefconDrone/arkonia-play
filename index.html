ARKONIA

Prototype v11 — full armies take the Field. Eighteen champions a side, eight kinds each, two ranks deep: pawns forward, archers and manticores on the flanks, the caster held at center. Sixteen unique champions in classic arcade pixels. Hold all five power points or destroy the enemy army.

Board: tap a piece, tap a highlighted square. Land on an enemy to challenge.
Eighteen a side: pawns forward (knights, goblins), ranged on the back flanks, the caster held at center.
Casters hold four spells, once each: Teleport, Revive, Imprison, Shift Time. The enemy caster casts too.
Wounds persist after battle and heal each turn — faster on ground that favors you. Hunt the wounded; shelter your own.
Arena vs AI — touch: drag anywhere to move; tap or hold the STRIKE button to attack — or flip 'auto' on and just pilot. Keys: WASD + Space.
Arena hotseat — Light: WASD + Space. Dark: Arrows + Enter. Touch: Light drags in the bottom half with a STRIKE button bottom-right; Dark drags in the top half with a STRIKE button top-right.
Fight on squares that favor your side — the favored champion enters stronger.
